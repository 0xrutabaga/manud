# MANUD name and brand policy

The MANUD code and Skill are open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). **The MIT License does not grant rights to the MANUD brand.** This page explains what you can and cannot do with the brand.

**Brand assets** means the names **MANUD** and **มะนุด**, the `manud.` wordmark (when published), the mascot **Little Manud / เจ้ามะนุด** and its files in [`assets/brand/`](assets/brand/), and any logos derived from them.

> Status: MANUD is not a registered trademark at this time. This policy states how the project asks its brand to be used, as an unregistered mark.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that your project uses, works with, is based on, or is a fork of MANUD ("based on MANUD", "a fork of MANUD").
- Use the names and the unmodified mascot to **refer to** this project: in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, package lists and links to this repository.
- Keep the brand assets in unmodified copies or forks of this repository, as long as the fork is clearly identified as a fork (see below).

## Please don't, without written permission

- Use **MANUD**, **มะนุด** or the mascot as the name or logo of your own product, fork, service, package, model or company, or in a way that suggests the MANUD project made or endorses it.
- Modify the mascot or wordmark, or create new artwork that could be confused with them, for your own branding.
- Use the brand assets on merchandise for sale.
- Register domain names, social handles, package names or app-store names containing MANUD/มะนุด that could be confused with this project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the MIT License. If you distribute a modified version publicly, please:

1. give it a different name (for example, not "MANUD" or "MANUD Pro"), and
2. replace the mascot and logo, or clearly mark the project as an unofficial fork.

You may keep a line such as "based on MANUD" in your README.

## Questions and permission

Open a GitHub issue titled "Brand use request". (Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.)
